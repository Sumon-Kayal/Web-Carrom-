How to Play

  • You play white coins, the computer plays black. Pot the red queen and it counts for whoever covers it.
  • Slide the striker left/right along your baseline before every shot — it can only be placed between the two baseline circles.
  • Drag back from the striker to aim, then release to shoot. Longer pull = more power.
  • Pot one of your own coins → you shoot again. Pot the opponent's coin → turn passes, coin is lost from play.
  • Pot the queen → you get one immediate follow-up shot to "cover" it by potting one of your own coins. Fail, and the queen is respotted at the centre.
  • Pot the striker itself → foul. Turn passes, and any coins you potted on that same stroke are returned to the board.
  • First player to legally pot all 9 of their own coins (with the queen properly covered) wins the game.
